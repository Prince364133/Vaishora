The opportunity is bigger than mehendi. You're looking at an on-demand beauty-services marketplace connecting customers with independent professionals who travel to homes, hotels, wedding venues and event locations.

One particular risk deserves attention: disintermediation.

Imagine you introduce a bride to a makeup artist. They complete the first booking. The bride gets the artist's number and books her again directly.

You have acquired the customer, paid to acquire her, and then lost the future transaction.

This is a documented structural challenge for service marketplaces, including Urban Company's repeat-user business. 

bsmedia.business-standard.com

Do not assume an app can solve this. You need to provide continued value through scheduling, customer protection, reliable replacements, payments and loyalty benefits.

8. The 45-day validation experiment
This is how I would actually test it.

Days 1–7: Supply acquisition

Find and speak with 30 independent artists.

Ask:

How many bookings do you get per month?

Where do customers find you?

How much do you spend on Instagram promotions?

How many enquiries become actual bookings?

Would you pay a commission for a confirmed customer?

Do you travel to customers' homes?

Days 8–14: Customer discovery

Interview 50 potential customers.

Speak to brides, recently married couples, wedding planners and people who organise events.

Don't ask whether they like your idea. Ask about their last actual booking, how they found the artist, what went wrong and how much they paid.

Days 15–25: Concierge marketplace

Build a simple website with artist portfolios, pricing and a booking request form.

Handle the matching and booking manually through WhatsApp.

Do not build a full application.

Days 26–35: Paid acquisition

Test Instagram ads, local SEO and referral partnerships.

Get actual paying customers, not free sign-ups or people saying they are interested.

Days 36–45: Unit economics

Calculate:

Customer acquisition cost.

Booking conversion rate.

Average booking value.

Marketplace commission.

Artist acceptance rate.

Cancellation rate.

Contribution per booking.

Repeat and referral booking rate.

9. The KPIs that decide whether you continue
Do not measure success by Instagram followers, registered artists or website visitors.

Measure actual transactions.
Metric	Pilot target
Artist interviews	20+
Verified artists onboarded	15–20
Qualified customer enquiries	50+
Completed paid bookings	15+
Artist acceptance rate	70%+
Successful fulfilment	85%+
Average customer rating	4.5/5
Commission	10–12%
CAC	₹250 or lower
Contribution after variable costs and CAC	Positive

These are founder-set thresholds. They are not published industry benchmarks.

My decision rules:
- GO
   If you reach 15 paid bookings, have reliable artists and positive contribution economics, invest in the MVP.
- PIVOT
   If customers enquire but don't pay, investigate pricing, trust and booking friction.
- STOP
   If you cannot secure sufficient supply or get at least 8 genuine paid bookings after 45 days of active outreach and marketing, don't pour more money into the same model.
One important distinction: 15 bookings validate initial demand, not product-market fit. You need repeated acquisition and positive economics over several months before scaling.
10. Operational rules you cannot compromise on
Since this is a managed home-service business, your reputation depends on the person who arrives at the customer's home.

Create these rules from day one:

Artist verification
- Identity verification.
- Phone verification.
- Portfolio authenticity.
- Reference checks.
- Skill assessment or trial.
- Clear service and travel pricing.
Booking reliability
- Artist confirmation before customer payment.
- Booking deposit and written cancellation terms.
- Backup artist for high-value events.
- Clear arrival and completion confirmation.
Customer safety
- Verified artist details shared before arrival.
- Consent before taking or publishing customer photographs.
- Hygiene and product standards.
- Complaint escalation and refunds.
- Appropriate home-visit safety procedures.
Payment management
- Maintain a booking ledger.
- Record the full transaction amount, commission and artist payout.
- Reconcile every booking.
- Don't let cash bookings disappear from your records.
